## WinterPaws - Per care website

---

### Purpose

A cozy winter companion platform designed for pet owners to ensure their furry friends stay warm, safe, and healthy during the cold season.

---


---

### Key Features👇

#### Home Page:

-   In the header, there is a logo, a menu, and a login button, with the active route highlighted in the menu.

-   The hero section features a slider with three items, showcasing images of pets wearing winter outfits.

-   In the popular winter care section, six of the most popular services are displayed.

-   The home page also includes winter care tips and information about three expert vets.

#### Services Page:

-   The service page displays 20 different services.

#### Profile Page:

-   This is a private route. To access this page, the user must be registered and logged in.

-   On this page, the user can see their photo, name, and email, along with an “Update Profile” button to edit their information. They can also view their recently booked services and details about their pets, including photos.

#### Update Profile Page:

-   This is a private route. To access this page, the user must be registered and logged in.

-   From this page, the user can change their name and profilepicture.

#### Register and Login Page:

-   If the user is new, they can complete registration on the registration page by providing their name, photo URL, email, and password.

-   If they are already registered, they can access the private routes by logging in through the login page using their email and password.

-   The user can also log in using their Gmail account if they wish.

#### Service Details Page:

-   By clicking the “View Details” button on the services page, the user can go to the service details page to see complete information about that service.

-   However, access to this page requires the user to be logged in. If the user is not logged in, they will be redirected to the login page. After successfully logging in, they will be redirected back to the desired service details page.

-   The user can book a service by providing their name and email.

#### Password Reset Request

-   If the user forgets their password, they can send a reset request. In this case, after entering their email and clicking the “Reset Password” button, a password reset link will be sent to their email.
-   Additionally, a new tab will open automatically, taking the user directly to their Gmail inbox.
